■About us

We model for the bench, not for the render.

Prong seats, stone seats, wall thickness and sprue placement get settled on the screen — not discovered at the bench afterwards. Patterns print at 25 µm in a wax-filled castable resin rated at 0.03% ash, so the detail stays crisp and the burnout stays clean.

Based in Burnaby, British Columbia. Five years of CAD drawing, a bench jeweller's background, and sculpture studied alongside both. Every design is original. English, Cantonese and Mandarin.

For shops in Metro Vancouver: I will come to you, with printed patterns and cast pieces in hand, so you can judge the surface yourself instead of from a photograph. Say when.
