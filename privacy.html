Privacy & Data

hikki photography & philosophy  ·  Burnaby & Victoria, British Columbia

This is a small independent photography practice, not an advertising business. The short version: this site does not track you, does not set tracking cookies, and does not send anything about you to an advertising network.

What this site does not do

  • — No tracking cookies, no advertising or re-targeting pixels.
  • — No third-party analytics that profile individual visitors.
  • — Your IP address is not sent to any location or profiling service.
  • — Nothing is sold, rented or shared for marketing.

What it does do

  • Approximate location. The menu shows a city and the local weather. The city is guessed from the time zone your browser already reports — no request leaves your device to work it out, and no identifier is stored.
  • Weather. Those coordinates (a city centre, not you) are sent to open-meteo.com to fetch a temperature. No personal data is included.
  • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which sees the request as any font host would.
  • Local storage. Small preferences (last reading, collapsed panels) stay in your own browser and are never transmitted.

If you send an enquiry

The enquiry form collects your name, email, city, enquiry type and message. It is delivered by Web3Forms straight to hikki.pnp@gmail.com and is used only to reply to you. It is not added to any mailing list. Ask at any time and your enquiry will be deleted.
